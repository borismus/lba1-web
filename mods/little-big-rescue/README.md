# Little Big Rescue

Zoe breaks Twinsen out of the Citadel prison. Play as Zoe: sneak in by the
side door, knock out the robot Rabbibunny guarding the stairs for the key to
the cell block, find a scientist's coat as a disguise, and get Twinsen out
of his cell on the crane lift. Then take the key from the guard by the
front doors, and face Funfrock, waiting in the courtyard outside.

A mod in the format of [docs/MODS.md](../../docs/MODS.md): it contains no
original game data, only operations on the player's own copy (`mod.ops`,
`levels/`), its own scripts (`scripts/`, some of them hooks into the
original's scripts) and its own voice clips (`voices/`, ElevenLabs).

- `levels/zoe_prison.txt`: a copy of the Citadel prison (scene 0).
- `levels/zoe_yard.txt`: a copy of the yard in front of it (scene 1).
- `mod.ops`: Zoe as the hero, and her disguise.

Requires GOG's *Twinsen's Little Big Adventure Classic*. Voices: ElevenLabs.
